re/read

Read articles and EPUB books offline, translate what you select, and grow a vocabulary in the language you read - all of it on this device. Nothing you read, select or save ever leaves it.

Turn the extension on in Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions, then allow it on the sites you read.

Free software, AGPL-3.0-or-later. Source: github.com/fundacja-reborn/reread-webext
