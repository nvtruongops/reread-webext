Offline reading list

How it works

Everything here is a text copy: web articles you opened in the reading view and deliberately saved, and the articles and .epub files you imported with Import below. They all open without a connection and stay on your device, even if the original page goes away.

Import and export (backup or EPUB)

      Import and export

          • The backup holds your articles with their highlights and reading positions, the saved phrases of every language pair, and the settings. Highlights made in books are in it too.
          • Books are in the backup only when you tick "Export with books"; otherwise keep your .epub files - when you import the same book again and the backup after it, its highlights come back.
          • Import reads the file it is given: a re/read backup (.zip - or the older .json files of the list and of the highlights), or a book (.epub).
          • To export only some of the articles or books, press Select above the list and tick them.
          File format

          • .zip - Import only adds what is missing and never overwrites what is saved: an article already here keeps its copy, a saved phrase keeps its meanings and takes only a sentence it lacks or a higher count, a highlight already here is left out.

            The settings are restored only when you tick "Restore the settings too".

          • .epub - the electronic book format. A book is stored as text and pictures, split into parts at import.
          • .md - notes with the highlights of articles and books, exported from the Highlights page.

          Highlights

          Export highlights

            Highlights travel in the backup of everything, exported and imported on the reading list - for books too.

            The .md file is a plain-text list of the quotes and notes, grouped by document - for reading and for your own notes. It cannot be imported.
